# NeighborNest

> Turning strangers into a genuine local crew.

NeighborNest solves relocation loneliness by engineering the exact conditions that produce authentic friendship — small matched groups, structured follow-through, and a fixed six-week cycle — instead of throwing newcomers into open events and hoping connection happens.

---

## 🧩 The Problem

Moving to a new city is easy. Making real friends there isn't.

- **Open events & meetups** → too big, too anonymous, no follow-through.
- **1-on-1 friend apps** → high pressure, one bad match and you're back to zero.
- **Local community groups** → low commitment, no accountability, easy to lurk and never connect.

Friendship needs **repetition, small groups, and a reason to keep showing up.** No existing platform engineers all three at once.

---

## 💡 The Solution

NeighborNest creates **closed Nests of 4–6 newcomers**, matched on values, life stage, and social goals — not just surface hobbies. One or two **local anchors** join each Nest to provide cultural context and a welcoming presence that accelerates trust.

Each Nest runs on a **structured six-week cycle**:

1. **Matching (Week 0)** — Deep compatibility matching + anchor assignment
2. **The Cycle (Weeks 1–6)** — Biweekly meetups with activity suggestions, icebreakers, and gentle nudges
3. **Vibe Check (Week 3)** — Anonymous check-in to catch mismatches early and re-match if needed
4. **Graduation (Week 6+)** — Join a new Nest, become an anchor, or stay connected via the alumni network
